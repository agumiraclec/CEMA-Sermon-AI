Questions churches ask first.

Something else? Ask us on WhatsApp.

How does the voice AI work during a service?

You speak into the microphone, for example "John 3:16" or "Next verse", and the screen updates. The pastor never has to look down, click a mouse or use a keyboard while preaching.

Do we need an internet connection?

Cloud AI gives the fastest, most accurate recognition. If your church has poor or no internet, the built-in Offline Mode works without Wi-Fi.

Will it work with our projector and live stream?

Yes. Project to your displays over a standard HDMI cable. For live streaming it connects to OBS, vMix and Wirecast through NDI and OBS output.

What if the preacher quotes a verse without the reference?

The software can recognise the quote and show the verse. If you prefer navigation by explicit reference only, turn this off in Settings.

Which Bible translations are included?

A large library including KJV, NKJV, NIV, ESV, NLT, AMP, NASB, NRSV and TPT, plus Hausa, Igbo, Yoruba and Nigerian Pidgin. Say the translation's name and it switches.

What do the AI sermon notes do?

They follow the message and capture key points, definitions, quotes and scriptures by topic. You can turn them into scenes, edit them, or export them after service.

Can we reuse our formatting on another computer?

Yes. Export all your output settings, background pictures included, as one file and import it on any computer. Your look stays the same every week.

Can I control it from my phone?

Yes. The phone remote connects over your local Wi-Fi with a 4-digit key. You can search verses, step through them and take scenes live from anywhere in the room.

How do I receive my activation key?

After you chat with us on WhatsApp and complete payment, we generate a secure activation key for your computer. Paste it into the app and you are ready.
